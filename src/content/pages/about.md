I'm Varshit — a security guy in Dubai.

I never picked a speciality. Since 2023 I've tested whatever application landed
in front of me: web, APIs, internal tools, the ones nobody wanted to own. That
turned out to be its own education. The same handful of mistakes keep showing
up, wearing a different framework each time.

Lately that includes AI agents, which are just applications with worse
boundaries.

This is where the research lands.
